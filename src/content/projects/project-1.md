description: |
    From Mockup to Market was a collaborative course with Smartbroker+, a Berlin-based neobroker, 
    exploring the design of innovative user interfaces for financial products. The course emphasizes translating 
    early-stage mockups into market-ready solutions, addressing real constraints of fintech environments.

    This work was produced in the winter semester 2025 as a study project at the Fachhochschule Potsdam in collaboration with Smartbroker AG. It represents an academic performance and not a commercial contract job.

        description: |
            The course was structured around several tasks that increased in complexity over time. 
            It began with a simple redesign of the Webview’s document page, followed by using customer feedback to improve the flow for creating a savings plan. 
            The third task focused on dividends, exploring how they could be presented more clearly in the app and which new features would add value. 
            The final and most extensive project centered on Personalization & Innovation, where, without any existing Voice of Customer input, my group chose to focus on AI implementation, a redesigned Explore tab, and a new Goal feature.
