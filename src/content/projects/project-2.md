description: |
    Tiltone is a motion controlled device, that offers new and innovative ways of interacting with sound by tilting and rotating the device.

    The device enables beginners a less intimidating entry into music, by giving them a more immersive way of interacting with music compared to traditional controls.

    This product was developed during the "Audio Interface Design" course led by Angelica Tavella in the Summer Semester 2025.
